How we work

Evidence before confidence.

Start with the decision

We define who will use a system, what decision it supports and how success will be observed.

Test the whole path

Models, tools, data flows and human review are evaluated together instead of as isolated components.

Leave an audit trail

Experiments produce versioned inputs, source-linked outputs and concise reports that another team can reproduce.
