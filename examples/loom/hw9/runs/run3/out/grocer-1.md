# This week's grocery list (grocer-1, freshcart), day 0

**Status: draft. Please confirm before I act on it.** LOOM marked this as a first request from freshcart, so nothing gets ordered or sent anywhere until you OK it.

Built around two health constraints from your LOOM record: **limit added sugar** and **prefer low-glycemic foods**. Amounts assume one person eating most meals at home for 7 days. Adjust as you like.

## Produce
- Leafy greens: spinach (1 large bag), mixed salad greens (1 box)
- Broccoli (2 heads), cauliflower (1 head)
- Bell peppers (4), zucchini (2), cucumbers (2)
- Cherry tomatoes (1 pint), onions (3), garlic (1 bulb)
- Mushrooms (8 oz)
- Avocados (3)
- Berries: blueberries and raspberries (1 pint each). Lower-glycemic fruit
- Apples (3), pears (2). Whole fruit rather than juice
- Lemons (2)

## Protein
- Eggs (1 dozen)
- Chicken breast or thighs (~1.5 lb)
- Salmon fillets (2)
- Firm tofu (1 block)
- Canned tuna or sardines in water/olive oil (2 cans)

## Dairy and alternatives
- Plain unsweetened Greek yogurt (32 oz). Sweeten with berries, not flavored cups
- Unsweetened almond or soy milk, or regular milk (half gallon)
- Cheese: feta or a block of cheddar (small)

## Whole grains and legumes (low-glycemic carbs)
- Steel-cut or rolled oats (not instant flavored packets)
- Quinoa (1 bag)
- Brown rice or farro (1 bag)
- 100% whole-grain or sprouted bread (1 loaf, check label for added sugar)
- Lentils, dry (1 lb)
- Canned chickpeas and black beans (2 cans each, low-sodium)

## Pantry
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Nuts: almonds and walnuts (unsalted, ~8 oz each)
- Chia or ground flax seeds
- Natural peanut or almond butter (ingredients: nuts, maybe salt, no added sugar)
- Canned crushed tomatoes (no sugar added)
- Spices: cinnamon, cumin, paprika, black pepper
- Plain or sparkling water, unsweetened tea/coffee

## Snacks
- Hummus (1 tub)
- Baby carrots, celery
- Plain popcorn kernels or air-popped popcorn
- Dark chocolate, 85%+ (small bar, optional)

## Skipped or swapped this week
- Soda, juice, sweetened teas → sparkling water, unsweetened tea
- Flavored yogurt → plain Greek yogurt + berries
- Sugary cereal, granola, instant flavored oatmeal → plain oats
- White bread, white rice → whole-grain bread, brown rice/quinoa/farro
- Bottled sauces and dressings with added sugar (ketchup, BBQ, teriyaki, many dressings) → olive oil + lemon, crushed tomatoes, spices
- Cookies, pastries, candy → nuts, fruit, a little dark chocolate

Label tip: check "Added sugars" on the nutrition panel; aim for 0-2 g per serving on staples like bread, sauces, nut butter and milk alternatives.
